Facilities

Our laboratory combines low-temperature measurement and microwave control with shared nanofabrication, characterization, and prototyping resources across Colorado School of Mines.

Lab Facilities

Measurement and control infrastructure in the Zhang Hybrid Quantum Systems Lab.

Expected early summer 2027

Bluefors LD250 Dilution Refrigerator

A dilution-refrigerator platform for low-temperature experiments on superconducting circuits, nanomechanical devices, quantum defects, and hybrid quantum systems.

Status: Expected to arrive in early summer 2027; not yet installed.

Available now

Microwave Measurement

Our laboratory currently operates a Copper Mountain Technologies vector network analyzer covering 100 kHz–9 GHz for microwave-device characterization.

Planned

Microwave Control and Readout

We plan to develop FPGA-based microwave control and readout systems for quantum measurement and control experiments.

Shared Facilities

Campus resources that extend our in-lab capabilities.

Shared Instrumentation Facility

Mines’ Shared Instrumentation Facility provides access to nanofabrication and materials-characterization capabilities, including cleanrooms, electron-beam and optical lithography, metal and thin-film deposition, and electron, scanning-probe, and optical microscopy.

Explore the SIF

Labriola Innovation Hub

The Labriola Innovation Hub offers makerspaces and specialized shops for creating, prototyping, testing, and iterating, including metal, wood, electronics, composites, and rapid-prototyping resources.
