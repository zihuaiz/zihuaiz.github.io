Join our group

We are always looking for motivated and talented scientists who are excited to explore the unknown.

How To Reach Out

Please email me at zihuaiz(at)berkeley.edu with a concise subject line such as Prospective Graduate Student - Your Name.

Current Openings

Postdocs No open position at the moment.
Graduate students One PhD student starting in Spring or Fall 2027. MS students at Mines are welcome to reach out at any time.
Undergraduate students Research opportunities are available for Mines undergraduates. Students from other institutions are also welcome to reach out about possible opportunities.

What To Include In Your Email

To help me understand your interests and respond thoughtfully, please include:

  • A brief description of the scientific questions or experimental directions that excite you, and why our group feels like a possible fit.
  • Your CV or resume, including research experience, coursework, technical skills, and publications if applicable.
  • Any questions you have about the group, the research, or possible paths to get involved.

Who Might Thrive Here

Our work sits at the boundary of physics, electrical engineering, and materials science. A strong fit may look different for different people, but useful instincts include:

  • Enjoying hands-on experimental work, careful troubleshooting, and learning new skills.
  • Being motivated by ambitious scientific ideas while valuing the technical details that make the science possible.

Curiosity and a willingness to learn matter more than prior experience.
