Join the group

Explore the unknown with us.

We are looking for thoughtful, motivated scientists who want to build quantum experiments, understand how devices really work, and learn across disciplinary boundaries.

How to reach out

Your note

What to include

  • A brief description of the scientific questions or experimental directions that excite you, and why our group feels like a possible fit.
  • Your CV or résumé, including research experience, coursework, technical skills, and publications if applicable.
  • Questions you have about the group, the research, or possible paths to get involved.
Our culture

Who might thrive here

  • You enjoy hands-on experimental work, careful troubleshooting, and learning new skills.
  • You are motivated by ambitious scientific ideas and value the technical details that make the science possible.
  • You are curious, collaborative, and willing to cross the boundaries between physics, engineering, and materials science.

Curiosity and a willingness to learn matter more than prior experience.
